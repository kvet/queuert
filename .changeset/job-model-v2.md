Redesign the job model around the head row. A chain is no longer an implicit grouping of job rows derived from its latest job: its head row _is_ the chain, and the chain's own facts — type name, status, deduplication key, trace context and completion time — live on that one row and are written when the chain ends rather than inferred. Alongside that, `blocked` becomes a first-class job status while dropping off chains, completed jobs surface `continuedToId`, the attempt triplet gains `attemptAt`, lease terminology folds into the attempt concept, and the listing APIs get status-dependent ordering.

**This is a breaking schema change that replaces the job tables rather than altering them. Your database must already be at v0.15.1 — `migrateToLatest()` refuses an older schema. Back up your database, stop all workers, then run `migrateToLatest()`: this is not a rolling deploy, job processing is unavailable while the upgrade runs, and old-version workers fail against the new schema.** The upgrade renames the live tables aside to `{tablePrefix}job_old` and `{tablePrefix}job_blocker_old`, installs the current schema from scratch, imports the rows a chain at a time, and drops the renamed tables once the row counts match; an interrupted run resumes from the last imported chain.

- **Status model** — `JobStatus` stays `"blocked" | "pending" | "running" | "completed"`. `ChainStatus` drops both `"blocked"` and `"pending"`, leaving `"running" | "completed"`: a chain is running until its last job completes terminally, so `Chain` is now a two-variant union and only the `completed` variant carries `output` and `completedAt`. A `blocked` job is one waiting on incomplete blocker chains: it is excluded from acquisition and moves to `pending` when the last blocker completes. Blocked jobs can now be rescheduled (`rescheduleJob` no longer throws `JobNotReschedulableError` for them; only `running` and `completed` jobs are rejected).
- **Chain navigation** — completed jobs now carry `continuedToId` (terminal variant: `continuedToId: null` with `output`; continued variant: `continuedToId: TJobId`, no `output`), so a chain can be walked forward from any job. `listChainJobs` cursors are now opaque id strings.
- **Attempt lifecycle** — a running job carries `attemptAt` (when the current attempt started) next to `attemptBy` and `attemptUntil`, distinct from `lastAttemptAt`. `LeaseConfig` → `AttemptConfig` (`leaseMs` → `timeoutMs`, `renewIntervalMs` → `heartbeatMs`), `leaseConfig` → `attemptConfig` on processors and worker defaults. Log events: `job_attempt_lease_expired` → `job_attempt_expired`, `job_attempt_lease_renewed` → `job_attempt_extended`, `job_reaped` → `job_attempt_reclaimed`. OTEL metrics: `queuert.job.attempt.lease_expired` → `queuert.job.attempt.expired`, `queuert.job.attempt.lease_renewed` → `queuert.job.attempt.extended`, `queuert.job.reaped` → `queuert.job.attempt.reclaimed`.
- **Listing APIs** — `listChains`, `listJobs` and `listChainJobs` take flat options with a discriminated union on `status` (a single string, not an array). `orderBy` is status-dependent and compile-time validated: for jobs, `blocked` and `pending` order by `createdAt` or `scheduledAt`, `running` by `createdAt`, `attemptAt` or `attemptUntil`, and `completed` by `createdAt` or `completedAt`; for chains, `completed` orders by `createdAt` or `completedAt` and `running` by `createdAt`. `root` is renamed `independent` on `listChains`, and `CreatedAtCursor` is renamed `TimestampWithIdCursor`.
- **Deduplication** — `scope` is now required (no implicit default). `scope: "incomplete"` renamed to `scope: "running"` (`"incomplete"` is no longer accepted).
- **Custom state adapters** — the `StateAdapter` contract is reshaped around what happens to a job rather than around the attempt that made it happen, and every method that returns a job now returns it paired with its chain. A custom adapter must be rewritten against the new contract; the conformance suite in `queuert/testing` is the checklist.
- **Migration locking** — concurrent `migrateToLatest()` calls are now safe across processes: the Postgres adapter serializes them via a new single-row `{tablePrefix}migration_lock` lease table, so exactly one pod performs the migration and the rest wait and skip (SQLite relies on its single-writer serialization).
- **Migration lineage** — the incremental lineage is collapsed to a single `001_initial_schema` migration that fresh installs and upgrades run alike; migration names are now `NNN_snake_case` rather than timestamps, and the 0.15.x records are removed from `{tablePrefix}migration` during the upgrade. Transient `{tablePrefix}job_old` and `{tablePrefix}job_blocker_old` tables exist for the duration of an upgrade and are dropped on success; on Postgres the `{tablePrefix}job_status` enum is dropped with them. `queuert/testing` no longer exports `seedAllStatesV1` or `SeedSentinelsV1`.
- **Schema** — `status` becomes `text` under a CHECK constraint (the Postgres enum is gone); `chain_status`, `chain_completed_at`, `continued_to_id` and `attempt_at` are added; `chain_type_name` is removed (a chain's type is its head job's `type_name`); `deduplication_key` is renamed `chain_deduplication_key` so every chain column shares the `chain_` prefix, and `leased_by` / `leased_until` become `attempt_by` / `attempt_until`. `chain_status`, `chain_deduplication_key` and `chain_trace_context` are written on head rows only, the foreign keys between job rows and from blocker rows to jobs are dropped, the blocker primary key includes its `index`, and the columns are reordered so fixed-width values stop paying alignment padding around the JSON payloads.
- **Indexes** — 10 become 11. `job_blocker_chain_idx` is unchanged, `job_chain_index_idx` becomes the partial `chain_index_idx` and `job_deduplication_idx` becomes `chain_deduplication_idx`; the seven acquisition-, lease- and listing-oriented indexes are replaced by one partial index per status — `job_pending_idx` (acquisition and pending listing), `job_blocked_idx`, `job_running_idx`, `job_completed_idx`, `chain_running_idx`, `chain_completed_idx` — plus the head-anchored `chain_idx` and the catch-all `job_idx`.
- **Dashboard** — the job list filters by `blocked` as its own status, and the Job Types view counts blocked jobs in their own column. The filter bar gains a status-dependent order-by and sort direction.
