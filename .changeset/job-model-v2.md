Redesign the job model: keep `blocked` as a first-class job status while dropping it from chains, surface `continuedToId` on completed jobs, add `attemptAt` to the attempt triplet, reshape the adapter's write surface around what happens to a job rather than around the attempt that made it happen, fold lease terminology into the attempt concept, and redesign listing APIs with status-dependent ordering.

**This is a breaking schema change that replaces the job tables rather than altering them. Your database must already be at v0.15.1 — `migrateToLatest()` refuses an older schema. Back up your database, stop all workers, then run `migrateToLatest()`: this is not a rolling deploy, job processing is unavailable while the upgrade runs, and old-version workers fail against the new schema.** The upgrade renames the live tables aside to `{tablePrefix}job_old` and `{tablePrefix}job_blocker_old`, installs the current schema from scratch, imports the rows a chain at a time, and drops the renamed tables once the row counts match; an interrupted run resumes from the last imported chain.

- **Status model** — `JobStatus` stays `"blocked" | "pending" | "running" | "completed"`, stored in a `status` column (`text` under a CHECK constraint; the Postgres enum is gone). `ChainStatus` drops both `"blocked"` and `"pending"`, leaving `"running" | "completed"`, stored on the head row in a new `chain_status` column. A `blocked` job is one waiting on incomplete blocker chains: it is excluded from acquisition and moves to `pending` when the last blocker completes. Blocked jobs can now be rescheduled (`rescheduleJob` no longer throws `JobNotReschedulableError` for them; only `running` and `completed` jobs are rejected).
- **Chain navigation** — completed jobs now carry `continuedToId` (terminal variant: `continuedToId: null` with `output`; continued variant: `continuedToId: TJobId`, no `output`). `chainIndex` stays on both the public `Job` type and the `StateJob` surface. `listChainJobs` cursors are now opaque id strings.
- **Attempt lifecycle** — `attemptAt` records when the current attempt started. Column renames: `leased_at` → `attempt_at`, `leased_by` → `attempt_by`, `leased_until` → `attempt_until`. Method renames: `acquireJob` → `startJobAttempt`, `renewJobLease` → `extendJobAttempt` (its `leaseDurationMs` is now `timeoutMs`), `reapExpiredJobLease` → `reclaimExpiredJobAttempt`, `getNextJobAvailableInMs` → `getStartAttemptDelayMs`. `LeaseConfig` → `AttemptConfig` (`leaseMs` → `timeoutMs`, `renewIntervalMs` → `heartbeatMs`), `leaseConfig` → `attemptConfig` on processors and worker defaults. Log events: `job_attempt_lease_expired` → `job_attempt_expired`, `job_attempt_lease_renewed` → `job_attempt_extended`, `job_reaped` → `job_attempt_reclaimed`. OTEL metrics: `queuert.job.attempt.lease_expired` → `queuert.job.attempt.expired`, `queuert.job.attempt.lease_renewed` → `queuert.job.attempt.extended`, `queuert.job.reaped` → `queuert.job.attempt.reclaimed`.
- **Migration locking** — concurrent `migrateToLatest()` calls are now safe across processes: the Postgres adapter serializes them via a new single-row `{tablePrefix}migration_lock` lease table, so exactly one pod performs the migration and the rest wait and skip (SQLite relies on its single-writer serialization).
- **Migration lineage** — the incremental lineage is collapsed to a single `001_initial_schema` migration that fresh installs and upgrades run alike; migration names are now `NNN_snake_case` rather than timestamps, and the 0.15.x records are removed from `{tablePrefix}migration` during the upgrade. Transient `{tablePrefix}job_old` and `{tablePrefix}job_blocker_old` tables exist for the duration of an upgrade and are dropped on success; on Postgres the `{tablePrefix}job_status` enum is dropped with them. `queuert/testing` no longer exports `seedAllStatesV1` or `SeedSentinelsV1`.
- **Listing APIs** — `listChains`, `listJobs`, and `listChainJobs` use flat options with a discriminated union on `status` (single string, not array). `orderBy` is status-dependent and compile-time validated. `root` → `independent`, `typeName` → `chainTypeName` on `listChainJobs`, `jobId` dropped from `listChains`. `CreatedAtCursor` renamed to `TimestampWithIdCursor`.
- **Index consolidation** — indexes reorganized from 10 to 12: `chain_index_idx`, `job_deduplication_idx` and `job_blocker_chain_idx` are unchanged, the seven status- and listing-oriented indexes are replaced by one partial index per status — `job_ready_idx` (`status = 'pending'`, acquisition and pending listing), `job_blocked_idx`, `job_running_idx`, `job_completed_idx`, `chain_running_idx`, `chain_completed_idx` — plus `chain_idx` and `job_idx`.
- **Deduplication** — `scope` is now required (no implicit default). `scope: "incomplete"` renamed to `scope: "running"` (`"incomplete"` is no longer accepted).
- **Dashboard** — the job list filters by `blocked` as its own status, and the Job Types view counts blocked jobs in their own column. Filter bar includes status-dependent order-by and sort direction. `listJobs` and `countByJobTypeNames` treat `blocked` as its own status (`status: "blocked"` orders by `createdAt` or `scheduledAt`, like `pending`).
