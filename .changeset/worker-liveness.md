The attempt lease moves from jobs to workers. Each in-process worker registers one worker at a time in the database, a row in a new `worker` table with its job types, its concurrency and a lease that one heartbeat per worker renews (`createInProcessWorker({ lease: { durationMs, heartbeatMs } })`, defaults 60s / 15s, with `heartbeatMs` below a third of `durationMs`), and a running job is live exactly while its worker's lease is live, so attempts carry no deadline and no per-job heartbeat. When a worker loses its lease it aborts its attempts' signals, waits for every attempt to end and registers again under the same id. An idle worker reclaims one expired worker at a time, of any job type, rescheduling its running jobs as a failed attempt with no backoff and deleting its row. The `002_worker_liveness` migration creates the `worker` table, adds `job.attempt_token`, drops `job.attempt_until` and returns every running job to pending, so stop every process on the previous version before running it.

- `attemptConfig` (on processors and `createProcessors`), the `AttemptConfig` type, the `defaults` option of `createInProcessWorker` and `InProcessWorkerDefaults` are removed; `createInProcessWorker` takes a top-level `backoffConfig` as the fallback for processors that don't set their own. `WorkerLeaseConfig` is exported.
- `Job.attemptUntil` is removed, also from `JobAttemptData` (the `job_attempt_*` logs and custom `ObservabilityAdapter` payloads), and running jobs are listed by `createdAt` or `attemptAt` only.
- Attempt-scoped writes are tied to the exact acquisition by an attempt token: `startJobAttempts` gives every claimed job a new random token, stored in the new `job.attempt_token` column and cleared when the job leaves `running`, so a stale attempt, from a slot that lost the job or from a chain deleted and recreated under the same id, fails its `complete` and writes nothing.
- The `workerName` option is replaced by `workerId`, the id the worker registers under and keeps across restarts. It defaults to the state adapter's new `generateWorkerId` option (a random UUID) and is checked with the new `validateWorkerId` option when the in-process worker is created, which throws the new `InvalidWorkerIdError`.
- While a live worker holds an id, another in-process worker with the same id waits for that lease to expire, reporting the new `WorkerAlreadyLiveError` through `workerError`; a lost lease is reported as the new `WorkerLeaseLostError`. A process that froze past its lease and wakes up after another one registered its id claims nothing and leaves the new row alone.
- `start()` registers the worker in the background, so `workerStarted` is emitted once its row is written, and a failed registration emits `workerError` and is retried after the `recoveryBackoffConfig` backoff. The `recoveryBackoffConfig` backoff covers failed registrations, loop errors and restarts, and resets when a job starts.
- `stop()` reschedules any job still running under the worker and deletes its row.
- An idle worker also reschedules its own running jobs that no attempt runs, left by a claim or a failed attempt's reschedule whose outcome was unknown.
- A reclaimed job records the failure: `lastAttemptAt` is set and `lastAttemptError` holds a serialized `JobAttemptReclaimedError` (new export, carrying `reclaimedBy`), and the dashboard shows it with the failed-last-attempt warning.
- `ObservabilityAdapter.jobAttemptReclaimed` and the `job_attempt_reclaimed` log carry `reclaimedFrom`, the worker the job was reclaimed from, and the log message is "Reclaimed job attempt".
- `workerLeaseLost` is added, with the `worker_lease_lost` log (warn) and the `queuert.worker.lease_lost` metric.
- Custom state adapters implement `startWorker` as a plain insert that gives the row a new random `token` and rejects a duplicate id, `extendWorkerLease`, `stopWorker`, `getWorkers` (with `lock: "exclusive"` and an `expired` flag computed in database time), `countWorkers`, `countWorkersByTypeName`, `countRunningJobsByWorkerId`, and the paginated `listWorkers` (with a `status: "live" | "expired"` filter) and `listWorkerJobs`, and expose `generateWorkerId` / `validateWorkerId` in `config`.
- Custom state adapters honour the optional per-job `attemptToken` of `completeJobs`, `continueJobs` and `rescheduleJobs`, and record the per-job `workerId` of `completeJobs` and `continueJobs` as `completedBy`.
- Custom state adapters: `extendJobAttempt` and `reclaimExpiredJobAttempts` are removed, and `startJobAttempts({ typeNames, workerId, workerToken, limit })` takes `typeNames` and must claim nothing unless the worker row exists with that token and a live lease (PostgreSQL takes `FOR KEY SHARE` on the worker row); `extendWorkerLease` and `stopWorker` take the same `workerId` and `workerToken`, which match only the row of that registration.
- `createPgStateAdapter` and `createSqliteStateAdapter` take a `workerIdType` option (default `uuid` / `TEXT`) for the worker ID columns; on PostgreSQL the migration recreates `job.attempt_by` and `job.completed_by` with that type, clearing the worker IDs of every job, `completedBy` of completed jobs included, without rewriting the `job` table; SQLite keeps the stored values.
- `StateAdapter`, `StateAdapterConfig`, `PgStateAdapter`, `SqliteStateAdapter` and `Job` gain a `TWorkerId` type parameter for worker IDs, right after `TJobId` (so `Job<TJobId, TWorkerId, TJobTypeName, …>` shifts the later parameters by one), so a job's `attemptBy` and `completedBy`, the client's worker methods and the `workerId` of `AttemptMiddleware`'s `wrapHandler` use the state adapter's worker ID type.
- PostgreSQL compares schedules and leases against the statement's time rather than the transaction's start, so a claim or a worker read inside a long transaction sees the current time.
- `Worker` is exported, with an `expired` flag computed in database time, and the client gains `getWorker`, `getWorkers`, `countWorkers`, `countWorkersByTypeName`, `countRunningJobsByWorkerId`, and the paginated `listWorkers` and `listWorkerJobs`.
- The dashboard adds a Workers list, worker pages and a Workers summary on the home page, links a running job's worker, finds workers by ID, warns on the home page about workers that lost their lease and job types with pending jobs and no live worker, and shows each job type's live and expired workers next to its name. A running job's deadline is now its worker's lease, and the "Deadline" sort is removed.
